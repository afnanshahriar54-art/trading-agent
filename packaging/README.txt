TRADING AGENT
=============

Open it: double-click "Trading Agent" on your Desktop or in the Start menu.

Everything uses PRACTICE money. Real-money trading is switched off.

How to use it
- Trade ideas: click "Find trade ideas", read each idea, click "Approve trade" if you agree.
- Open trades: check once a day; close trades the agent suggests closing.
- How it works: your safety rules, a glossary, and an "Open settings folder" button.

Connect your Alpaca practice account (optional)
1. Click "Open settings folder" in the app (How it works page).
2. Open the file ".env" with Notepad and paste your Alpaca paper keys after
   ALPACA_API_KEY= and ALPACA_SECRET_KEY= . Save.
3. Open "config.yaml" with Notepad. Under us_stocks, change "broker: paper" to "broker: alpaca". Save.
4. Close and reopen the app.

Keep ".env" private. Never share it.

Uninstall: run "Uninstall Trading Agent" from the Start menu, or delete this folder and the
"Trading Agent" shortcuts.
