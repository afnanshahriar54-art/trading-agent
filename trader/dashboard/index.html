What to do

  1. Go to Trade ideas and click Find trade ideas. The agent checks every market in about 10 seconds.
  2. Read each idea: what it would do, how much you could lose, and why. Approve the ones you agree with.
  3. Or open Charts, watch the live price, and place your own practice trade.
  4. Check Open trades once a day. If the agent suggests closing one, you'll see it there.

4. Safety rules (both modes)

Every trade must pass these, whether you or the app places it.

5. Take profit

Close a winning trade automatically as soon as it reaches this gain. At Alpaca (US stocks) the target sits at the broker and closes instantly, even when this app is closed. On the practice accounts on this PC, it's checked every 30 seconds while the app is open, and at the daily check. Applies to new trades.

Trade-off, from the report card: a take-profit wins more often and has smaller drops, but makes less money overall, because it cuts off the big winners. Over the past ~4 years at 2× risk: US stocks +30% instead of +38%, crypto +12% instead of +23%.

6. Protect profits (lower risk)

Moves a trade's safety net in your favour as it gains, so a winning trade can't turn into a big loss. A safety net only ever moves up (down for short trades), never back. "R" = what the trade risked when it opened. Works on all accounts: at Alpaca the safety-net order itself is moved.

Report card, past ~4 years: trailing at 1.5× R did best. Crypto made +34% instead of +23% with a smaller worst drop (−15% instead of −19%); US stocks were about the same profit (+39%) with a slightly smaller worst drop. Break-even alone helped a little. The fixed take-profit above lowered profits.

7. Forex trade size (trade ideas and Auto mode)

How big the agent makes forex trades. Your own trades on the Charts page use the amount you type there.

A fixed amount is never allowed to risk more than the risk rule would. The broker holds only a small deposit (margin) per trade, about 3.3% of its size on the practice account.

Methods

A "method" is a fixed rule for when to buy and sell, for example "buy when the price breaks above its 20-day high". The agent uses the methods that are switched on here to find your trade ideas. Switched-off methods are ignored.

Build your own method

Your method buys when all of the "buy when" conditions are true, and sells when any "sell when" condition is true. With no sell conditions, it sells as soon as the buy conditions stop being true. Your methods only buy; they never sell short. They start switched off: test first, then switch on.

How it works

The short version, in plain words.

Your safety rules

    These are in config.yaml. Your account keys go in .env. Both are in the settings folder; open them with Notepad.

    Words you'll see

    Practice account
    Fake money at a real broker (or on this computer). Real prices, no real risk.
    Confidence (0-100)
    How good the idea looks, combining the method's track record, how it did lately, whether other methods agree, and whether today's market suits it. The agent only trades ideas at or above your minimum.
    Safety net (stop-loss)
    A price at which the trade is closed automatically to limit the loss.
    Sell short
    Betting a price will fall. You profit if it goes down and lose if it goes up.
    Method
    A fixed rule for when to buy and sell, e.g. "buy when the 12-day average price rises above the 26-day average".
    Track record
    How the method would have done on this asset over the past years, after fees. Past results don't guarantee future ones.
